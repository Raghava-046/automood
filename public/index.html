Frequently Asked Questions

What is AutoMood?+

AutoMood is an AI voice-calling platform that answers, makes, and manages phone calls for businesses — handling everything from lead qualification to appointment booking.

Can it make outbound calls?+

Yes. AutoMood can place outbound calls for follow-ups, reminders, and sales conversations on your behalf.

Can it answer incoming calls?+

Yes. Every incoming call is answered instantly, day or night, with no hold time.

Can it qualify leads?+

Yes. It asks the questions your team would ask, then routes qualified leads to you.

Can it book appointments?+

Yes. It checks your calendar and books appointments directly during the call.

Can it work 24/7?+

Yes. AutoMood never sleeps, so no call goes unanswered — even outside business hours.

What businesses can use AutoMood?+

Any business with frequent customer calls — real estate, healthcare, education, travel, dealerships, contractors, and local services.
